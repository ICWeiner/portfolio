# Vision

This project exists to demonstrate my ability to:

- Build software
- Deploy software
- Operate software
- Document software
- Maintain software

The website is only the presentation layer.

The project is the entire system.

---

## Why does this project exist?

To prove, with a live example, that I can take a service from an idea to a
production system running on my own infrastructure — and keep it running.

## Who is it for?

- Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating my skills.
- Other engineers who want to see how a small system is built and operated.
- Future me, who will look back to understand what was built and why.

## What should a recruiter learn after 5 minutes?

Who I am, what I have done, and that I ship and operate production software —
reachable at my own domain over HTTPS, deployed automatically, and running a real
backend.

## What should another engineer learn after 15 minutes?

That the code, the deployment pipeline, the infrastructure, and the documentation
are treated as first-class, versioned, reproducible engineering artifacts.

## What should future Diogo learn after 2 years?

How and why each decision was made — visible in the ADRs — and what running this
platform taught me. The record of what worked and what did not.
